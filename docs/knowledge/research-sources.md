# Research data sources

What the research loaders (`src/obliskiq/research/data.py`) read from each vendor, as seen when
the fixtures in `tests/fixtures/research/` were recorded. The endpoints are configuration
(`config/defaults/research.yaml`); this file records their observed formats and what they do
not promise.

| Vendor | Observed | Point in time |
|---|---|---|
| Yahoo through `yfinance` 1.7.0 | `Ticker(...).history(auto_adjust=False, actions=True)` returns a frame indexed by the session's timestamp at midnight in the exchange's zone (`Europe/Berlin` for `EUNL.DE`, `America/New_York` for `SPY`), with `Open`, `High`, `Low`, `Close`, `Adj Close`, `Volume`, `Dividends`, `Stock Splits`, `Capital Gains`; `Close` is split-adjusted, `Adj Close` also dividend-adjusted; an accumulating fund's two closes are equal. A direct `curl` to Yahoo's chart endpoint answered HTTP 429; `yfinance` warned that its crumb fetch was rate limited and still served the history `[Yahoo ✓ 2026-10-16]` | as observed (the close of its session); not point in time across splits or dividends, which re-scale history backwards |
| Ken French data library | a zip holding one CSV: free-text header lines, then tables that each open with a row starting with a comma (`,Mkt-RF,SMB,HML,RF`) and end at a blank line; the first is monthly (`YYYYMM`), the second annual (`YYYY`); values in percent; the file names the CRSP vintage it was built from ("created using the 202608 CRSP database"); missing values are `-99.99` or `-999` in the portfolio files `[Dartmouth ✓ 2026-10-16]` | revised without vintages; availability is a declared lag |
| AQR data sets | an `.xlsx` workbook; the data sheet (`TSMOM Factors`) has notes above a header row (row 18), Excel serial dates in column A (1900 system: 31078 is 1985-01-31) and decimal excess returns; other sheets hold definitions and disclosures `[AQR ✓ 2026-10-16]` | revised without vintages; availability is a declared lag |
| FRED | `fredgraph.csv?id=<series>`: header `observation_date,<series>`, one row per observation, `.` for a missing value; the latest revision only `[FRED ✓ 2026-10-16]` | a declared lag after the observation date |
| ALFRED | `fred/series/observations` with `realtime_start` and `realtime_end` (and `FRED_API_KEY`): one row per observation and vintage with `date`, `value`, `realtime_start`, `realtime_end`; UNRATE for October 2023 was first published at 3.9 on 2023-11-03 and revised to 3.8 on 2024-01-05. The answer does not echo the key `[ALFRED ✓ 2026-10-16]` | vintages: each value public from its `realtime_start` |
| ECB reference rates | `eurofxref-hist.zip`: one CSV, newest date first, a trailing comma on every row, units of each currency per euro, `N/A` for a currency not fixed (legacy currencies); no row on TARGET holidays (Good Friday and Easter Monday 2024 are absent) `[ECB ✓ 2026-10-16]` | as observed (published around 16:00 CET on its day) |
| MSCI end-of-day index levels | `getLevelDataForGraph?currency_symbol=EUR&index_variant=NETR&start_date=YYYYMMDD&end_date=YYYYMMDD&data_frequency=DAILY&index_codes=990100` answers JSON (`msci_index_code`, `index_variant_type`, `ISO_currency_symbol`, `indexes.INDEX_LEVELS[]` of `calc_date` as the integer YYYYMMDD and `level_eod`); a start before 1997-01-01 is refused with `error_code` 100; levels begin on 2000-12-29 whatever the start asked for, for World (990100), Emerging Markets (891800) and ACWI (892400) in EUR and USD; calculation days include weekday holidays (1 January 2024 has a level) `[MSCI ✓ 2026-10-17]` | as observed (the end of each calculation day); restated without vintages |
| ECB Data Portal | `data-api.ecb.europa.eu/service/data/<flow>/<key>?format=csvdata` answers one CSV row per period with `KEY`, `TIME_PERIOD`, `OBS_VALUE` and quoted metadata columns; EONIA (`EON/D.EONIA_TO.RATE`) runs from 1999-01-04 to 2021-12-31, the euro short-term rate (`EST/B.EU000A2X2A25.WT`) from 2019-10-01; the AAA yield curve (`YC/B.U2.EUR.4F.G_N_A.SV_C_YM.SR_10Y`) from 2004-09-06 `[ECB ✓ 2026-10-17]` | as observed (EONIA in the evening of its day; the euro STR at 08:00 the next TARGET day) |
| Bundesbank time-series database | `api.statistiken.bundesbank.de/rest/data/BBSIS/D.I.ZST.ZI.EUR.S1311.B.A604.R<NN>XX.R.A.A._Z._Z.A?format=csv&lang=en`: a byte-order mark, a header naming the series, metadata rows (unit `Prozent`), then `YYYY-MM-DD,value,flag` with `.` and "No value available" on days without one; residual maturities of 1 to 20 years from 1997-08-07, 25 and 30 years from 2000-08-01. The rates are zero-coupon and continuously compounded: the 1-, 5- and 10-year rates of 2026-10-05 (2.91, 3.18, 3.51) equal the Svensson formula of that day's published parameters (β0 4.15209, β1 −1.89415, β2 0.00541, β3 −2.72620, τ1 0.46454, τ2 2.16227) to two decimals `[Bundesbank ✓ 2026-10-17]` | as observed (estimated from the day's closing prices; published afterwards) |
| Yahoo, European listings | the histories of `EUNL.DE`, `IWDA.AS`, `IQQW.DE`, `IWRD.L` and the iShares and Lyxor euro bond lines on Xetra, Amsterdam and Paris start in 2008–2009 whatever the fund's launch; `XMWO.DE` and `MTX.PA` are not found; `CRSOX` is now the Cantor Fitzgerald (formerly Credit Suisse) Commodity Return Strategy Fund, from 2004-12-31; `^SPGSCI` is the S&P GSCI headline index, from 1990 in the first read and from 1984-01-03 with `history(period='max')` in a later one (which variant was not established, so it is not used) `[Yahoo ✓ 2026-10-17]` | as observed |
| Fund metadata | Yahoo's `netExpenseRatio` (percent) on 2026-10-17: EUNL 0.20, EUNM 0.18, CBE7 0.20, IBGL 0.20, XEON 0.10, IUST 0.10, SXRM 0.07, CMOD 0.19; `totalAssets` (USD) where served: EUNM 10.49 bn, IS3N 46.80 bn, VGVF 11.61 bn, VFEM 5.99 bn, CBE7 1.35 bn, IBGL 1.01 bn, SXRM 4.94 bn, XEON 24.20 bn. iShares' product page gave IWDA's net assets as USD 151.80 bn and its total expense ratio as 0.20 % on 16 October 2026 `[Yahoo ✓ 2026-10-17]` `[iShares ✓ 2026-10-17]` | metadata, read once |
| MSCI country indices | `searchIndexes?index_scope=Country&index_size=12&index_style=None&index_suite=C` (same host and path as the levels) lists 129 standard country indices with their codes; the levels endpoint serves every current MSCI ACWI country from 2000-12-29 except Qatar, the United Arab Emirates and Kuwait (2005-06-01) and Saudi Arabia (2014-09-01), and Argentina, Pakistan, Jordan and Morocco, which left MSCI ACWI, still from 2000-12-29; Russia and Venezuela, removed in 2022 and 2006, are not served under any code tried (the search lists only "Russia Value", which serves nothing) `[MSCI ✓ 2026-10-17]` | as observed; restated without vintages |
| SEC insider transaction data sets | the "Insider Transactions Data Sets" page lists 83 quarterly `<yyyy>q<n>_form345.zip` files, 2006Q1 to 2026Q3, under `/files/structureddata/data/insider-transactions-data-sets/` (from 2026Q2 under `/files/datastandardsinnovation/data/insider-transactions-data-sets/`), about 15 MB each; each holds tab-separated `SUBMISSION`, `REPORTINGOWNER`, `NONDERIV_TRANS`, `DERIV_TRANS` and holding and footnote tables keyed by `ACCESSION_NUMBER`, dates as `DD-MON-YYYY`, `DOCUMENT_TYPE` 3, 4, 5 and their amendments, `TRANS_CODE` (P purchase, S sale, A grant, F tax withholding, M exercise), `RPTOWNER_RELATIONSHIP` as a comma list (`Director,Officer`), and `ISSUERTRADINGSYMBOL` as filed (`NONE`, `N/A`, or several classes). Requests need a declared User-Agent (`SEC_USER_AGENT`) `[SEC ✓ 2026-10-17]` | as filed: `FILING_DATE` is the day the filing was accepted (no time of day) |
| SEC company tickers and XBRL frames | `company_tickers_exchange.json` maps today's CIKs to tickers and exchanges (10,444 rows), with no history; `data.sec.gov/api/xbrl/frames/us-gaap/<tag>/USD/CY<yyyy>[Q<n>I].json` gives one value per filer, the last one filed, so a restatement replaces the original `[SEC ✓ 2026-10-17]` | the frames are not point in time; the Financial Statement Data Sets (from 2009Q2) carry each filing's own date |
| Yahoo, delisted US stocks | a symbol Yahoo no longer lists raises `YFTzMissingError` ("possibly delisted; no timezone found"), answers HTTP 404 "Quote not found" or an empty body; a reused symbol serves its new owner's history (`SHLD` from 2023-09-14). After about 1,700 rapid symbols through `yf.download` in threads Yahoo throttled silently (empty frames, no error); one `Ticker.history` per symbol with `yf.config.debug.hide_exceptions = False`, from four processes, went through at 100 symbols per 20 to 60 seconds `[Yahoo ✓ 2026-10-17]` | survivors only |
| Tiingo ticker list | `apimedia.tiingo.com/docs/tiingo/daily/supported_tickers.zip`, no key: 109,070 tickers with exchange, asset type, currency and first and last dates; delisted US stocks are listed with their end dates, 95 % of those that ended before 2026 in 2014 or later `[Tiingo ✓ 2026-10-17]` | a list; prices need a key |
| Yahoo, long-history US funds and futures | `history(period='max')` first rows: `GC=F` (gold futures, continuous front month) 2000-08-30, `CEF` (gold and silver trust) 1986-04-03, `PCRIX` (PIMCO CommodityRealReturn, institutional) 2002-07-01, `VIPSX` (Vanguard inflation-protected) 2000-06-29, `PRRIX` (PIMCO Real Return) 1997-01-29, `VFITX` (Vanguard intermediate Treasury) 1991-10-28, `VUSTX` (Vanguard long Treasury) 1986-05-19, `IAU` 2005-01-28, `DJP` 2006-10-30; `QRAAX`, `^BCOM` and `^BCOMTR` are not found `[Yahoo ✓ 2026-10-17]` | as observed |
| ECB Data Portal, more flows | convergence 10-year government yields `IRS/M.<country>.L.L40.CI.0000.EUR.N.Z`, monthly: Italy from 1991-03, Spain from 1991-11; the euro-area HICP index `ICP/M.U2.N.000000.4.INX` from 1996-01; the composite indicator of systemic stress `CISS/D.<area>.Z0Z.4F.EC.SS_CIN.IDX`, daily from 1980-01 for the euro area (`U2`) and the US `[ECB ✓ 2026-10-17]` | as published now; the CISS is recomputed without vintages |
| OECD SDMX | composite leading indicators `OECD.SDD.STES,DSD_STES@DF_CLI` (amplitude adjusted, monthly), 22 areas: the US, Canada, Japan and the UK from 1960-01, Germany 1961, Italy 1961, France 1962, Australia 1966, South Africa 1975, Mexico 1982, Spain 1985, Turkey 1987, Brazil 1989, Korea 1990, China 1992, Indonesia 1994, India 1994, and aggregates `[OECD ✓ 2026-10-17]` | latest revision only; vintages not probed |
| BIS Data Portal | central-bank policy rates `WS_CBPOL` through `stats.bis.org/api/v2/data/dataflow/BIS/WS_CBPOL/1.0/M.<country>?format=csv`; Japan monthly from 1946-01 `[BIS ✓ 2026-10-17]` | as published now |
| Japan Ministry of Finance | `jgbcme_all.csv`: JGB yields by maturity (1 to 40 years), daily from 1974-09-24, `-` where a maturity was not quoted `[MoF ✓ 2026-10-17]` | as observed |
| Ken French, international | `Developed_3_Factors_CSV.zip` is monthly from 1990-07, built from Bloomberg data ("created using the 202608 Bloomberg database"), missing values `-99.99`; the Europe and Emerging files answer `[Ken French ✓ 2026-10-17]` | revised without vintages |
