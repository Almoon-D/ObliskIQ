# The tone an LLM reads in issuers' press releases adds to the price signal (H7)

A forward test: its evidence is produced live by the EventExtractor and exists only from its
first run. Not investment advice.

## Mechanism and citation

Tetlock (2007) and Tetlock, Saar-Tsechansky and Macskassy (2008) find that the negative words in
news about a firm predict its earnings and its returns over the following days, and that prices
under-react to them; Lopez-Lira and Tang (2023) find that a language model's reading of headlines
predicts next-day returns, more strongly for small firms and after negative news. The mechanism is
limited attention: the content of a release is absorbed over days, not at its publication. The
evidence for language models is contaminated wherever the model was trained after the news it
reads (Glasserman and Lin 2023), which is why this test counts only outputs produced live
(DECISIONS D70).

## Universe

The nine issuers with an EDGAR company feed in `config/sources.yaml` at this merge: Freeport-
McMoRan, Albemarle, Mosaic and Bunge (8-K), and Cameco, Nutrien, ASML, Banco Santander and
TotalEnergies (6-K). They were chosen in Session 10 as the satellite names whose filings read the
same way, before any extraction existed. Nine names make a small sample; the power section says
what that costs.

## Data and their point-in-time status

An **event** is an extraction with status `ok` of an item from one of those feeds, produced by the
segment above: model `gemini-3.1-flash-lite` of the `gemini` provider, prompt version 1, prompt
hash `5bed4ad9…ad77` (the hash of the instructions, the item template, the output schema and the
extractor's parameters; `obliskiq llm status` prints it). An output of another model or prompt is
not in this sample. The item's text is the filing's press release, cut to the extractor's
`max_item_chars`. When two events of one issuer fall within the horizon of each other, only the
first counts (`overlap`), so no issuer's outcomes overlap.

For each event:

- **entry**: the close of the first session of the issuer's listing venue that ends after the
  output was produced (the calendars of `calendars/`), so nothing after the output is known at
  entry;
- **outcome**: the log return from the entry close to the close `horizon_sessions` = 5 sessions
  later, minus the log return of the benchmark `IWDA` (the core's world equity vehicle, in its
  listing currency) from its close on or before the entry date to its close on or before the
  exit date;
- **reaction** (a control): the issuer's log return from its last close before the filing was
  published to the entry close, the market's response already in the price;
- **momentum** (a control): the issuer's log return over the `momentum_sessions` = 20 sessions
  ending at its last close before the filing was published.

Closes are the IBKR daily bars the collectors store, as observed. An event without the closes it
needs is not scored; the adequacy check counts them.

## Primary metric and horizon

β, the slope on tone of the ordinary least squares regression of the outcome on tone, reaction
and momentum (with an intercept), over the scored events: the five-session excess log return a
unit of tone adds beyond the price signal. Its interval [L, U] at level 1 − 2α (α from
`research.decision.alpha`, 0.05) comes from a moving-block bootstrap over event dates (2,000 paths,
blocks of 10 consecutive event dates, seed 20261023), so events of one date move together (D63).
The outcome follows D62: sufficient if L ≥ θ, insufficient if U < θ, inconclusive otherwise.

The sample runs from the segment's first output to its **evaluation date**: the later of
2027-10-29 and twelve months after that first output, fixed now so that a late first run does not
shorten it. The outputs are not examined against returns before then.

## Theta and why

θ = 0.01: a wholly positive reading (tone 1) adds at least 1 % over five sessions. Typical tones
are about ±0.5, so at θ a typical event adds 0.5 %, just over the 0.4 % round trip that
`costs.yaml` assumes for a liquid share. Below θ a trade on a typical event does not pay for
itself, and the feature would not earn a place beside the price signal.

## Power

Inputs stated before any output: the five-session excess log return of these large caps has a
standard deviation near 4 % (a daily 1.8 % over five sessions), tone near 0.5, and about 300
events are expected by the evaluation date (nine issuers, near three press releases a month
each). The standard error of β is then about 0.04 / (0.5 × √300) ≈ 0.0046, so the power to detect
θ (the chance that L exceeds zero when β = θ, at α = 0.05, as `research/stats.py` computes it) is
about 0.70, below the target 0.8; the effect detectable at 0.8 is about 0.0115. With the 150
events the adequacy check requires, the power is about 0.45. An inconclusive outcome under that
power is reported as such and is never read as evidence against (D62).

## Adequacy

Not evaluable, and nothing concluded, when on the evaluation date fewer than 150 events are
scored, or fewer than 95 % of the events in the segment have the closes they need. The evaluation
date does not move; a later test needs a new pre-registration.

## Adjustment

One test, no adjustment. There is no development sample and no exploratory trial: the trial
registry holds none for this id.

## Robustness checks

- The sign of β in each half of the sample (split at the median event date).
- The placebo: each event's outcome regressed on the tone of the same issuer's previous event (at
  least the horizon earlier) with the same controls; its slope must show nothing.
- The long-only expression: buying at the entry close when tone is above zero and selling at the
  exit close, net of each name's friction tier in `costs.yaml`; its break-even cost multiplier
  must exceed the declared cost uncertainty of 2 (the next tier up, D63).
- Reported, not decisive: β with novelty times tone as the feature.

## Trial budget

Zero.

## What a sufficient, insufficient or inconclusive outcome would lead to (D62)

- **Sufficient**: the extracted tone joins the Hunter's evidence for these names in shadow
  (Session 16 builds the layer on this series), then an exploration budget (D65).
- **Inconclusive, positive**: shadow at zero weight, and the evidence of later outputs of this
  segment pooled with this sample by the same method.
- **Inconclusive otherwise**: the extractor keeps collecting (it is cheap); the feature consumes
  nothing.
- **Insufficient**: the hypothesis is retired with its record; the extractor's outputs stay for
  the other roles of the layer.
- **Not evaluable**: data work (more names with press releases, a longer window) under a new
  pre-registration.
